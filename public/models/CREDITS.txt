robot.glb
  "RobotExpressive" by Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius), modifications by Don McCurdy.
  License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Used for NULL's walker and brute robots. The game recolours it and merges its parts
  into one skinned mesh at load time.

Everything else you see in the game (city, cars, people, drones, turrets, signs, effects,
music and sound) is generated in code by this project.
